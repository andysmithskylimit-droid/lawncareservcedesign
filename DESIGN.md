# JCP Lawn Service — Garden living

## Direction
A modern California garden, presented as a miniature living world. A large interactive garden model earns the visual attention; useful, quiet navigation and generous botanical typography surround it.

## Tokens
- Evergreen #163E30 — headings and primary actions
- Meadow #527646 — planting accents
- Mist #EDF3E7 — hero and gentle section fields
- Paper #FAFBF6 — main background
- Sunflower #E7EF80 — small tactile action accents
- Stone #6C766C — secondary text
- Manrope for all text, weight 400–750; headings use tight tracking and generous proportions.

## Composition
Desktop: floating navigation; left-aligned 44% hero text / 56% live garden; service section with large changing photograph opposite an expandable service list; broad photographic interlude; three-step contact process; map and contact form; useful FAQs; oversized brand footer.
Mobile: compact menu; headline before garden; in-flow scene controls; stacked service photos/list and form. No pinned scroll traps.

## Plan critique
Avoided generic dark neon lawn branding and testimonial/statistics blocks. Kept the signature detail specific to lawn care: a miniature living lawn with daylight, sunset, wind, and rotation. Services have purposeful interaction rather than identical feature cards. No invented ratings, experience, prices, or customer projects.

## Motion
One coordinated hero entrance, slow garden wind, GSAP-led image parallax and section choreography, Lenis scrolling. All motion respects reduced-motion preference and an explicit pause control. Touch and keyboard controls remain usable.
