## Self-hosted type

Manrope from Google Fonts, normal 400, 500, 600, 700, and 800 weights, delivered in `dist/assets/manrope-{weight}.ttf`. Optional ready-to-use declarations are in `dist/assets/fonts.css`. Include with a stylesheet link to `./assets/fonts.css`, then use `font-family: Manrope, sans-serif`.

Font stylesheet source: `https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Manrope:wght@400;500;600;700;800&display=swap`.

Original Manrope font license is included as `dist/assets/Manrope-OFL.txt`; source: `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/manrope/OFL.txt`.

Downloaded October 14, 2026. Assets are served locally and require no third-party image or font requests at runtime.
